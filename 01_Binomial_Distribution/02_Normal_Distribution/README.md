# Experiment 2: Normal Distribution

## Objective
To study the Normal Distribution and calculate probabilities for different intervals.

## Experiments
1. Mean = 0, Standard Deviation = 1.
2. Mean = 0, Standard Deviation = 2.
3. Mean = 1, Standard Deviation = 1.

## Learning Outcome
Understood how mean and standard deviation affect the shape of the normal distribution and probabilities over an interval.
