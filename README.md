
# Experiment 2: Normal Distribution

## Objective
To study the effect of mean and standard deviation on the normal distribution.

## Experimental Observations

| Experiment | Mean | Sigma | Probability |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0 | 1 | 0.68269 |
| 2 | 0 | 2 | 0.38292 |
| 3 | 1 | 1 | 0.47778 |

## Observations

1. With mean 0 and standard deviation 1, the probability between -1 and 1 was 0.68269.
2. Increasing standard deviation to 2 made the curve wider and flatter.
3. Increasing mean to 1 shifted the curve towards the right.

## Conclusion

The mean determines the centre of the normal distribution, while standard deviation determines its spread. Both parameters affect the probability within a given interval.
